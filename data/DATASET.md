`id` prefixes record which prompt produced an email (`ack-gen-*`, `meet-gen-*`,
`faq-gen-*`, `sup-gen-*`, `emo-gen-*`, `amb-gen-*`, `spam-gen-*`). They do
**not** give the label: trap cases carry a label that differs from their
generating prompt.

with the system prompt in `app/handlers/llm.py`, `max_tokens=300`.
